LinkedIn Starter Checklist

Six things to finish today. Then one comment a week — that's the whole ongoing job.

Do these six before you leave

  • Upload a real photo. Your face filling the frame, good light, plain background. A phone is fine.
  • Rewrite your headline. Use the formula below. This line follows you everywhere on LinkedIn.
  • Claim your custom URL. Edit public profile & URL, top right of your profile. You get to change it once.
  • Write three paragraphs in About. What you're doing now · what you've actually done · what you want next.
  • Add one experience with a real description. Jobs, class projects, clubs, volunteering — all of it counts.
  • Send ten connection requests. Always with a note. See the template below.

The headline formula

What you do + who you do it for + what you're after
Student→ Design student building brand identities for local businesses
Seeking opportunities→ Studying cybersecurity · looking for a summer internship in Northwest Ohio
Cashier at Kroger→ Customer service and inventory · studying business at Defiance College

The connection note

Copy this shape

“Hi [name] — we met at the Junior Achievement workshop today. I'm studying [x] at [school] and would like to stay connected.”

A blank request is a coin flip. Two sentences makes it almost automatic — it answers the only question they have.

Then: comment, don't stress about posting

A real comment on someone else's post puts your name and headline in front of their whole network. You don't have to write anything original.

Add something — a question, an experience, a disagreement. Two sentences is plenty. “Great post!” is invisible.
